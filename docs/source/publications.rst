Projects using OpenAirClim
--------------------------

- `Non-CO₂ MRV <https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/transport-decarbonisation/reducing-emissions-aviation_en>`__:
  OpenAirClim is used for small emitters (Method D) within the European
  Commission's Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (MRV) framework for
  non-CO₂ emissions from aviation. The model is integrated into the
  `NEATS <https://neats.eurocontrol.int/>`__ tool, under development by
  EUROCONTROL.
- `DINA2030+ <https://www.dlr.de/en/vt/latest/news/six-institutes-join-forces-to-improve-the-wet-engine-concept>`__:
  Project funded by the Bundesministerium für Wirtschaft and Klimaschutz under
  the German Aviation Research Program LuFo VI-2. The development of the
  OpenAirClim contrail module was partially financed by this project.
- `HEROPS <https://www.nlr.org/newsroom/case/rd-case-hydrogen-electric-propulsion-for-climate-neutral-regional-aviation/>`__:
  The Hydrogen-Electric Zero Emission Propulsion System (HEROPS) project aims
  to introduce climate-neutral propulsion into regional aircraft. OpenAirClim
  is being used by Royal NLR to quantify the climate impacts of future aircraft
  powered by hydrogen.
- `Impact Monitor 2 <https://impactmonitor.eu/im2/home>`__: The project aims to
  deliver a coherent and holistic toolbox and framework for climate impact
  assessments. OpenAirClim is integrated into the modelling framework.
- `328H2-FC <https://www.dlr.de/en/el/research-and-transfer/projects/current-projects/do328>`__:
  Project funded by the Bundesministerium für Wirtschaft and Klimaschutz under
  the German Aviation Research Program LuFo VI-2. The development of the
  OpenAirClim contrail module was partially financed by this project.
