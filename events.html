Events

Since development began in 2022, OpenAirClim has quickly evolved into a flexible framework for the analysis of aviation climate impacts. The model is already in use in research, industry and policy alike, contributing to a more sustainable future for aviation.

To bring the community together, we have established the OpenAirClim Symposium. This event allows developers, researchers, policymakers and industry representatives to learn about recent scientific advances, discuss ideas and gain hands-on experience with the framework.

Important

Abstract submissions for the OpenAirClim Symposium 2027 are now open! The deadline is January 15, 2027. Please visit the symposium website.

OpenAirClim Symposium 2027

Bridging science, software and sustainable aviation.

We are pleased to announce the first OpenAirClim Symposium! The two-day event will take place on April 13-14, 2027 at the DLR campus in Oberpfaffenhofen, near Munich. It will feature a blend of scientific presentations, collaborative discussions and a hands-on workshop for new users.

Key Information

  • Date: April 13-14, 2027

  • Location: DLR Oberpfaffenhofen, Münchener Str. 20, 82234 Weßling, Germany

  • Abstract Submission: open until January 15, 2027. Selected contributions will undergo peer review and with the possibility of publication in JECATS

  • Registration: January 29 - March 12, 2027
